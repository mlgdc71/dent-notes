# Developmental grooves
> main grooves that connect [[Cusps]]
> formed where adjacents [[Developmental Lobe|lobes]] at the base of [[Cusps]] join during tooth development

# supplementary grooves
RATHER THAN connecting cusps
- these grooves are **==smaller==** and run **==within==** a cusp
- define characteristics on actual [[Cusps]]

>Supplementary grooves facilitate the joining of other grooves and may merge or connect with **==masticatory structures==** or **==marginal ridges==**, sometimes appearing as a **==triangular bridge==** or connecting to the margin bridge.

# groove naming
grooves are named based on their position:
- central
- buccal
- lingual

AND also the **==cusp==** they associate with

# pits and fissures
![[Pasted image 20260325224345.png]]

>anatomical anomalies within the groove not fully fused or [[Developmental Lobe|lobes]] during development
# pits
>Pit --> localised ==hole== in the groove/[[Fossae]]

- specific points of defect in tooth formation (when incomplete)
- associated with **==cuspal or groove development==** and groove communication

# fissures
>Fissures --> **==sharp==** defect with a ==gap==

- narrow, linear cleft
- also caused by incomplete fusion of enamel or [[Developmental Lobe|lobes]]
