Ridge identification
# ridge naming
named by the:
- cusp tip
- direction surface is facing
- cusp it is associated with

e.g. --> buccal ridge of lingual cusp

# transverse
>the ridge that connects two cusps/triangular ridges across the **occlusal** surface, forming an ==elevated bridge of support==

- pathway between cusps

# Marginal
>A **locally elevated ridge** on the edge of a tooth that helps form the **==margin (TERMINAL BOUNDARY along LATERAL EDGES) of the occlusal==** or ==**lingual surface**== dentition

- provides structural integrity
- will be removed if diseased

# cervical ridge
>subtle ridge running medio-distally along cervical region of ALL dentition

- **==most prominent==** on permanent molars and primary teeth
- located at the junction between enamel-covered crown and root

IMPORTANCE
--> it helps prevent ==**crack propagation**== from the occlusal surface down into the root
