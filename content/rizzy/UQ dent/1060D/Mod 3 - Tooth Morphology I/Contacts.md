# occlusal contact
>refers to the intricate relationship of contact between opposing dentition (**==occlusal or incisal surface==**)--> influenced by anatomy

- ==occlusal surface grooves== facilitate guiding and grinding of food during chewing
- contact when **==cusp tip==** or **==[[Ridges|ridge]]==** of one tooth meets **==central [[Grooves|groove]]==** of opposing tooth in proper occlusion
	- IDEAL ALIGNMENT OF OCCLUSION
	- occurs during ==**lateral movement**== of the jaw

# contact points
![[Screenshot 2026-03-25 at 9.53.44 pm.png]]

>the contact point is an arbitrary point in the upper third of the [[The Tooth Crown|tooth]] where the most **==bulbous==** part of adjacent teeth touch

- not necessarily the highest or lowest point
- "region of greatest contour on **==mesial==** or **==distal surfaces==** where teeth contact"
	- supports gingiva and **==provides stabilisation==**

![[Pasted image 20260325224425.png]]

THE PURPOSE of the contact point is to:
- minimise food impaction
- protect gingiva
- support the stability of dental arch
