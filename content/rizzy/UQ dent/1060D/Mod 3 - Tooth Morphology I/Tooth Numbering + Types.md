# Teeth Surfaces
buccal --> closest to cheek
labial --> surface of anterior closest to the lip
palatal --> inner of maxillary teeth facing palate
lingual --> inner of mandibular teeth facing tongue
mesial --> tooth surface in contact with midline of the face, towards centre of dental arch
distal --> away from midline, away from centre of dental arch
incisal --> anterior teeth ==cutting== edge
occlusal --> posterior teeth ==chewing== surface

# FUNCTIONS OF DIFF TYPES OF TEETH + basic characteristics
![[Pasted image 20260319205507.png]]
## Incisors
- cut food
	- due to flat, sharp edges
- is the longest
- central + lateral --> names

## Canine
- grasp and hold food
- ==**pointed cusps**==

## Premolar
- transition between canines and molars
- aids in grasping and grinding

# Molars
- crush and grind food
	- withstand the forces during chewing
- greatest volume
- 1,2,3 --> names
![[Pasted image 20260319205459.png]]

>Transition from incisor to molar
>--> root **==elongates==**
>--> crown becomes **==multi-headed==**
>--> crown design increases in **==complexity==**
>--> cusps no longer thin
>--> no longer narrow like **==incisal edges==**
