# Tubercle
>a local prominence of enamel formed on some portion of the crown --> commonly on molars

appears as a mini cusp or step

![[Pasted image 20260319205346.png]]


"­It always forms on the palatal surface of the mesio-palatal cusp on the 1st maxillary molar (Cusp of Carabelli)"

## commonly found
on the ==mesoppalatal cusp of maxillary molars==

# Mammalons / mamelon
--> found in anterior teeth

typically have M shape and are ==present at eruption==

![[Pasted image 20260319220500.png]]

>over time they are **==ground away==** and usually lost due to wear from grinding and occlusion
