# Developmental grooves
> main grooves that connect [[Cusps]]
> formed where adjacents [[Cusps]] join

# supplementary grooves
RATHER THAN connecting cusps
--> these grooves are **==smaller==** and run **==within==** a cusp

>Supplementary grooves facilitate the joining of other grooves and may merge or connect with **==masticatory structures==** or **==marginal ridges==**, sometimes appearing as a **==triangular bridge==** or connecting to the margin bridge.

# groove naming
grooves are named based on their position:
- central
- buccal
- lingual

AND also the **==cusp==** they associate with

# pits and fissures
>defects within the groove not fully fused

Fissures --> **==sharp==** defect with a ==gap==
Pit --> localised ==hole== in the groove
