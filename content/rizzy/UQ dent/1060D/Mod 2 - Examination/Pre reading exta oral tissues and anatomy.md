>The lateral pterygoid muscle (the pink coloured muscle) is the muscle that connects both the mandible and the TMJ to the posterior border of the upper jaw (maxilla). This allows for anterior (forward) movement of the TMJ and lateral movements.

## Medial peryoid muscle
SIDE TO SIDE
protrusion
--> not backward

>The medial pterygoid muscle is the final muscle of mastication which connects the medial surface of the lower border of the mandible to the maxillary and palatine bones. This muscle also allows for anterior movement of the mandible.

- The **medial pterygoid** is a muscle involved in mastication (chewing).
- It primarily **elevates the mandible** (closes the jaw) and **assists with side-to-side movements**.
- The **movement facilitated by the medial pterygoid is not anterior movement**; instead, it is mostly elevation.
- The **anterior movement** of the mandible is usually associated with the **lateral pterygoid**.
- **Common mistake:** Confusing the function of the pterygoid muscles; remember their roles in chewing and jaw movement.
