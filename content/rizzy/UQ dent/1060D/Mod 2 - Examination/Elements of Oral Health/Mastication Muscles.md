> temporalis and masseter muscles are the primary muscles responsible for mastication (chewing).

# Lateral Pterygoid Muscle
## Connections
>**The lateral pterygoid muscle connects the mandible and the TMJ to the pterygoid process of the sphenoid bone**

1. Connects the ==condylar neck of mandible==  to the ==temporomandibular joint== (TMJ)
2. Attaches the ==pterygoid plate== of the ==sphenoid bone==
3. NOT MAXILLA

The LPM connects directly to the cartilage disc of the TMJ

## Jaw Movement
- Depression (Down)
- Protrusion (Forward)
- Lateral Movement (Side to side)

# Medial Pterygoid Muscle
## Connections
>attaches from the ==medial surface of the mandibular ramus== to the _==pterygoid plates (sphenoid) of the sphenoid bone==_, **not** just anywhere on the mandible.

**main attachment points** --> _medial surface of the mandibular angle and the mandibular posterior part_.

ts like a bridge to inner jaw
## Jaw Movement
> Facilitates anterior movement of the mandible
