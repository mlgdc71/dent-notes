# Salivary Glands
>==**parotid**, **submandibular**, and **sublingual**== glands.

- These glands are responsible for producing saliva to aid digestion and oral health.
## Size Comparison
The LARGEST --> is parotid gland (most prominent and widest)

--> everyone else is smaller
- 🚩 **Mnemonic**: "P for Peak (largest), S for Smaller".
# Tonsils
>==**lingual**, **palatine**, and **pharyngeal**== tonsils.

- **Adenoids** refer to the **pharyngeal tonsils**; they are not a different type of tonsil.
- The **tubal tonsils** are minor and not considered one of the main types.
# Gingiva
>Healthy gingiva is characterized by **pink** color, normal size, firm attachment, no bleeding or exudate, and well-defined architecture.
