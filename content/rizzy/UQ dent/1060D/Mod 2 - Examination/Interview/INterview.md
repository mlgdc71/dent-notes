# Symptoms + Signs
[[Symptoms]]
==Symptoms== are subjective experiences reported by the patient e.g:
- pain
- nausea


==Signs== are objective findings observed by the clinician e.g:
- swelling
- rash

# Co-existing Pain
e.g. **headache** + **fibromyalgia**

--> impacts dental treatment

- it is **imperative** to evaluate these conditions as it helps better **oral health management** and ==**more effective treatment planning**==
- ==holistic diagnosis== --> including chronic pain conditions

# Spectrum of disease severity
>Ranges from mild to severe illness caused by infection

## Immunocompromised patients
--> the spectrum is ==wider== or expanded
--> they experience a broader range of disease outcomes

Their ==**immune response variability**== means they may tolerate different **levels of microbial exposure** at different times.
