# Zones
the dental environment is organised into specific zones to maintain ==cleanliness and prevent cross-contamination==
1. office areas
2. reception
3. staff room
4. waiting room
5. contaminated treatment zone

--> these contaminated zones divide into clean and dirty zones --> control infection risk

>**Proper zoning** is essential for ==**infection control**== and efficient clinic workflow.
