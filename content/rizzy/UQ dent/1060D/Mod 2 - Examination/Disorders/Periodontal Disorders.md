# Periodontitis
>Periodontitis is characterized by inflammation extending ==beyond the gingiva== into the **ligaments** and bone, which causes destruction of attachments.

**Periodontitis damages supporting ligaments and bones, not just the mucosa or gums.**

# Furcal Involvement
#memorise 
Bone loss in the furcation space of multi-rooted teeth

>Loss of bone attachment in the furcal space between roots of multi-rooted teeth.
