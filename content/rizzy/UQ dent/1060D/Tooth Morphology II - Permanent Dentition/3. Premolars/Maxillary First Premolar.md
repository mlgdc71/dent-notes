# buccal
- romboid bell-shaped outline
- shorter occluso-cervically
	- relative to [[Maxillary Canine]]

![[Pasted image 20260331183904.png]]

# palatal
- palatal cusp is shorter
	- more convex
		- seen from occlusal
- straightening surface near the cervical line

![[Pasted image 20260331184152.png]]

# mesial
- quadrilateral
- arrowhead fusion

# distal
- quadrilateral
- distal is more smaller and more regular

# occlusal
- ovoid
- broader than [[Maxillary Canine]]
- more prominent buccal cusp
- mesial marginal ridge with central groove

![[Pasted image 20260331184419.png]]

- single root
	- or 2 root
- first premolar more likely to have 2 roots
