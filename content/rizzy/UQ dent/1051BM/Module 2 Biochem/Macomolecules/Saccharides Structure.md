>The **structure and function** of polysaccharides are determined by their **sugar monomers and the glycosidic linkages**.


# Glycosidic Linkage
- A **glycosidic linkage** is the **bond between two monosaccharides**.

# Monosaccharides

Monosaccharides are sugars that may be aldehydes or ketones, and they vary in the length of their carbon skeleton.

- Monosaccharides are simple sugars that can be classified based on the type of carbonyl group they contain.
- These sugars vary in the length of their carbon skeleton, typically ranging from **3 to 6 carbons**.
- Remember, ==**aldose** monosaccharides== contain an aldehyde group, while **ketose** monosaccharides contain a ketone group.
