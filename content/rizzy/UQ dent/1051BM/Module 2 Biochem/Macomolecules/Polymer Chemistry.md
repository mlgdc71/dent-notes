# Enzymes
>They play a crucial role in ==both== **polymer synthesis** (building polymers) and **polymer breakdown** (degradation).

- Remember: enzymes are **specific** to the reactions they catalyze. In polymer chemistry, certain enzymes are specialized for either synthesis or breakdown, but generally, they can do both depending on the context!

# Hydrolysis
- **Hydrolysis** is the reaction that **breaks down polymers into monomers**.

# Dehydration
- **Dehydration (condensation) reactions** are responsible for **forming polymers from monomers**.

💧 Think "dehydration" as **removing water to join molecules** (forming bonds). In contrast, **hydrolysis adds water to break** bonds. Memorize that dehydration = building polymers, hydrolysis = breaking them down. This simple rule helps avoid common mix-ups.
