# Cellulose
- Humans lack the enzyme to digest **β(1→4) glycosidic bonds** found in cellulose.
- Humans can digest **α(1→4) glycosidic bonds** found in amylopectin.
- The ability to digest certain polysaccharides depends on the presence of specific enzymes.
- **Cellulose** is indigestible because humans cannot break β(1→4) bonds.
- **Amylopectin** is digestible because humans can break α(1→4) bonds.

- Think: **"α" bonds are Accepted (digestible), β bonds are Banned (indigestible)"** 😋🚫
- Remember **amylopectin = α(1→4)** bonds, and humans can digest those.
- Remember **cellulose = β(1→4)** bonds, and humans cannot digest those.

**🌿 Cellulose = β, no enzymes → indigestible** **🍠 Amylopectin = α, enzymes present → digestible**
