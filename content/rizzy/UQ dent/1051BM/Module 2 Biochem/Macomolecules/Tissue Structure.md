
- Epithelial cells are specialized cells that form the lining of surfaces and cavities in the body.
- These cells contain specific proteins that provide structural support and resilience.
- **Keratin** is a key protein found in epithelial cells, especially in skin and related tissues.
- **Collagen** is a structural protein primarily found in connective tissues like skin, bones, and cartilage, not primarily in epithelial cells.
- **Common mistake:** Confusing collagen (connective tissue) with keratin (epithelial tissue).
