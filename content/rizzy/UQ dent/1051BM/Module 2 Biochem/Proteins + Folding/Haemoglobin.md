# Function
- is a protein found in ==red blood cells==
- **MAIN FUNCTION**
	- transport **oxygen** from lungs to body tissues (coronary system)
	- also other way --> CO2 from ==coronary to respiratory==

# Amino Acid Substitution --> solubility
>Amino acid substitutions on the **surface** of hemoglobin can ==influence its **solubility**.==
>ESPECIALLY in its ==deoxygenated form==

>Replacing a hydrophilic (water-loving) amino acid with a hydrophobic (water-repelling) one can create a ==**hydrophobic patch**==

e.g Glu (hydrophilic) to Val (hydrophobic)
--> thus ==reducing== solubility AND **==aggregation of haemoglobin==**

**NOT ALL SUBSTITUTIONS** affect solubility

# e.g. Val Beta-6 mutation
- causes formation of ==hydrophobic patch==
	- in turn promotes ==**aggregation of deoxyhemoglobin**==
	- hydrophobic amino acids promote aggregation by **sticking together**
- the mutation leads to **HYDROPHOBIC** not **HYDROPHILIC** interactions
