# kcat
> how fast can an enzyme work?

- The **turnover number (kcat)** measures the **maximum number of substrate molecules** converted to product **per enzyme molecule per unit time**.
- It indicates **enzyme efficiency** in catalyzing a reaction.
- The **correct answer** focuses on the rate at which a _single enzyme molecule_ can convert substrate to product.
- A common mistake is to interpret it as an overall efficiency or speed, rather than the rate **per enzyme molecule**.
