# RTK
> "They primarily phosphorylate tyrosine amino acid residues to transduce signals"

- **Receptor tyrosine kinases (RTKs)** are enzymes involved in [[Cell Signalling]]
- ==RTKs primarily regulate **metabolism, cell growth, and cell reproduction**.==
- They are critical for various cellular processes, including ==proliferation and differentiation==.
- Think of RTKs as **growth and reproduction** regulators — like **receivers** that activate signals for cell **growth, division, and reproduction**.

# Adenylyl Cyclase
- **Adenylyl cyclase** is an enzyme involved in cell signalling.
- It **converts ATP** into **cyclic AMP (cAMP)**.
- are activated by G proteins to produce cAMP which propagates [[Cell Signalling|cell signals]] from hormones
 
# Kinases + Phosphatases
**Kinases add** phosphate groups, **phosphatases remove** them
