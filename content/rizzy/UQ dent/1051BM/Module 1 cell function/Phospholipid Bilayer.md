# Model Reasoning
- The **fluid mosaic model** describes the **cell membrane** as a **dynamic, flexible structure**.
- It consists of a **phospholipid bilayer** with **embedded or attached proteins**.
- These **proteins and lipids** can **move laterally**, giving the membrane its **fluid** nature.
- This model helps **==explain==** **membrane functions** such as [[transport]] and ==**cell signaling**.==

# Fluidity

>Membrane fluidity refers to the viscosity of the lipid bilayer, influenced by unsaturated hydrocarbon tails with ==kinks==, cholesterol content, and saturation of tails.

# Membrane Functions
- Membrane functions include **signaling**, **enzymatic activity**, **transport**, **recognition**, and **attachment** to the ==cytoskeleton or extracellular matrix.==
- [[Exo + Endocytosis|Receptor-mediated endocytosis]] involves **==ligand binding**, **coated pits**==, and **vesicle internalization**.
- Cell membranes consist of **phospholipids**, **proteins**, **cholesterol**, and **carbohydrates**;
    - **Phospholipids** form a bilayer
    - [[Transport|Proteins]] perform various cellular functions
    - **Cholesterol** regulates fluidity
    - ==**Carbohydrates** are important for cell recognition==
