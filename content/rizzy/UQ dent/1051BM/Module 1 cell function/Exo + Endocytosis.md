## Exocytosis + Pinocytosis + Phagocytosis
>The process where intracellular vesicles fuse with the ==plasma membrane== to release contents outside the cell. #memorise 

- **Endocytosis types:** include _phagocytosis_ (cell eating solid particles), _pinocytosis_ (cell drinking liquids), and _receptor-mediated endocytosis_ (specific molecules are taken up via receptors).
- ==**Pinocytosis** involves the nonspecific uptake of fluids, not solids.==
- **Phagocytosis** is specialized for large solid particles.

- A common mistake is to think of exocytosis only as waste removal; it also includes secretion of vital molecules.
	- it also releases hormones and enzymes

## RME
**Receptor-mediated endocytosis** involves **specific receptors** on the cell surface that **bind ligands**, leading to vesicle formation for **internalization.**
