## ⭐ 2. **Skull Landmarks & Openings**

### **Definitions**

- **Fissure** → narrow slit between bones
    
- **Foramen** → tube‑like opening for nerves/vessels
    
- **Canal** → long tube‑like passage (e.g., mandibular canal)
    
- **Fossa** → shallow depression
    
- **Sulcus** → groove for vessels/nerves
    
- **Meatus** → opening into a canal (e.g., external auditory meatus)
    

### **Articular & Projection Terms**

- **Condyle** → articulating projection (mandible, skull base)
    
- **Facet** → smooth articular surface
    
- **Crest** → sharp ridge
    
- **Spinous process** → slender projection
    
- **Tubercle** → small rounded projection
    
- **Tuberosity** → large roughened projection
    

## ⭐ 3. **Cranial & Facial Bones**

### **Cranial (Neurocranium)**

- Frontal
    
- Parietal (paired)
    
- Temporal (paired)
    
- Occipital
    
- Sphenoid
    
- Ethmoid
    

### **Facial (Viscerocranium)**

- Maxilla (paired)
    
- Mandible
    
- Zygomatic (paired)
    
- Nasal (paired)
    
- Lacrimal (paired)
    
- Palatine (paired)
    
- Inferior nasal concha (paired)
    
- Vomer
    

### **Paired vs Unpaired**

- **Unpaired**: frontal, ethmoid, sphenoid, occipital, vomer
    
- **Paired**: parietal, temporal, maxilla, zygomatic, lacrimal, palatine, inferior nasal concha
    

## ⭐ 4. **Cranial Sutures & Fontanelles**

- **Coronal** → frontal–parietal
    
- **Lambdoid** → parietal–occipital
    
- **Metopic** → between frontal bones (fuses <1 yr)
    
- **Anterior fontanelle** → between frontal + parietal bones (infants)
    

## ⭐ 5. **Cranial Fossae**

- **Anterior**, **Middle**, **Posterior** cranial fossae
    
- Middle fossa foramina: **rotundum**, **ovale**, **spinosum**
    

## ⭐ 6. **Zygomatic Arch & Nasal Complex**

- **Zygomatic arch**: zygomatic bone + temporal bone
    
- Nasal structure: nasal bones, maxillae, palatine bones, ethmoid, vomer, inferior conchae
    

## ⭐ 7. **Mandible Essentials**

- **Condyle** → TMJ articulation
    
- **Coronoid process** → muscle attachment
    
- **Mandibular notch** → landmark for nerve blocks
    
- **Mental foramen** → mental nerve exit
    
- **Mandibular foramen** → inferior alveolar nerve
    
- **TMJ** → synovial joint with hinge + slide
    

## ⭐ 8. **Muscles of Mastication**

- **Temporalis** → elevate + retract mandible
    
- **Masseter** → elevate mandible
    
- **Medial pterygoid** → elevate + lateral movement
    
- **Lateral pterygoid** → **protrusion** + depression
    
- **Innervation**: V3 (mandibular branch of trigeminal)
    

## ⭐ 9. **Muscles of Facial Expression**

- **Orbicularis oris** → lip closure, “kissing muscle”
    
- **Buccinator** → compress cheeks
    
- **Innervation**: CN VII (facial nerve)
    

## ⭐ 10. **Oral Cavity Regions & Landmarks**

- Borders: lips, cheeks, palate, floor, posterior oropharynx
    
- Key features: nasolabial groove, philtrum, vermilion border, vermilion zone
    
- **Muco‑gingival junction** → attached gingiva vs alveolar mucosa
    
- **Alveolar process** → tooth‑bearing bone
    
- **Vestibule** → between lips/cheeks & teeth
    
- **Vestibular fornix** → deepest reflection

## ⭐ 12. **Palate**

### **Hard Palate**

- Maxilla (palatine process) + palatine bones
    
- Foramina: **incisive**, **greater palatine**, **lesser palatine**
    

### **Soft Palate**

- Muscles: palatoglossus, palatopharyngeus, musculus uvulae
    
- Innervation: mostly **CN X**, except palatoglossus (**CN X**)
    
- Function: closes nasopharynx during swallowing
    
- Contains: uvula, palatine tonsils, arches
    

## ⭐ 13. **Tongue Anatomy**

### **Muscles**

- **Extrinsic**: genioglossus, hyoglossus, styloglossus, palatoglossus
    
- **Intrinsic**: shape changes
    
- **Motor innervation**: CN XII (except palatoglossus → CN X)
    

### **Papillae**

- **Filiform** → tactile
    
- **Fungiform** → taste
    
- **Foliate** → taste (posterolateral)
    
- **Circumvallate** → V‑shaped row, bitter taste
    

### **Innervation**

- **Anterior 2/3**:
    
    - Sensory → V3 (lingual)
        
    - Taste → CN VII (chorda tympani)
        
- **Posterior 1/3**:
    
    - Sensory + taste → CN IX
        
- **Motor** → CN XII
    

### **Lingual tonsil**

- Immune tissue at tongue base
    

## ⭐ 14. **Pharynx & Larynx**

- Pharynx divisions: **naso‑**, **oro‑**, **laryngopharynx**
    
- Oropharynx: from hard/soft palate junction → epiglottis
    
- Contains: palatine tonsils, posterior pharyngeal wall, base of tongue
    
- Larynx includes: epiglottis, glottis, vocal folds
    

## ⭐ 15. **Salivary Glands**

- **Parotid** → Stenson duct (opens opposite upper 1st molar)
    
- **Submandibular** → Wharton duct
    
- **Sublingual** → multiple ducts along floor of mouth
    
- Produce enzymes: **amylase**, **lipase**
    

## ⭐ 16. **Cranial Nerves (Key Functions)**

- **CN V** → facial sensation, mastication
    
- **CN VII** → facial expression, taste (ant 2/3)
    
- **CN IX** → taste + sensation (post 1/3)
    
- **CN X** → palate muscles, pharynx
    
- **CN XII** → tongue motor
    
- **Total CNs**: 12
    

## ⭐ 17. **Hyoid Bone**

- Suspended U‑shaped bone
    
- Supports tongue + larynx
    
- Muscles: stylohyoid, hyoglossus, genioglossus
    
- Location: below mandible, above larynx
