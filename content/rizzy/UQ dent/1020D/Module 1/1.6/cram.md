mandible --> only moveable bone of the skull

medullary cavity of long bones
- The **medullary cavity** is the central hollow region within long bones.
- The **endosteum** is a thin membrane lining the medullary cavity.
- The **perichondrium** surrounds cartilage, not bone.
- The **==periosteum==** covers the outer surface of bones, but does **not** line the medullary cavity.

PERIOSTEUM --> fibrous membrane covering outer surface of bones

foramina
- The **middle cranial fossa** contains the foramina: **foramen rotundum**, **foramen oval**, and **foramen spinosum**.
- Other foramina like the **optic canal**, **superior orbital fissure**, and **foramen lacerum** are located in different parts of the skull.
- The **foramen magnum**, **jugular foramen**, and **carotid canal** are found in the posterior and other regions.
