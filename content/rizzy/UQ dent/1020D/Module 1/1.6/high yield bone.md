## ⭐ 1. **Bone Basics**

- Bone = **organ** (multiple tissues, functions).
    
- Functions: **support**, **movement**, **protection**, **mineral storage**, **blood cell production**, **homeostasis**.
    
- Resists **compressive forces** best.
    
- Adult skeleton: **206 bones**.
- axial --> 80

## ⭐ 6. **Bone Extracellular Matrix**

- **Inorganic**: hydroxyapatite → hardness
    
- **Organic**: collagen → tensile strength
    
- Water + mineral salts
    

## ⭐ 7. **Bone Cells**

- **Osteoprogenitors** → from mesenchymal stem cells
    
- **Osteoblasts** → form osteoid (bone matrix)
    
- **Osteocytes** → trapped osteoblasts in lacunae; maintain bone
    
- **Osteoclasts** → resorb bone; from hematopoietic lineage
    

## ⭐ 8. **Osteoclast Activity**

- Create **acidic environment** → dissolves minerals
    
- Release **cathepsin K** → breaks collagen
    
- Sit in **Howship’s lacunae**
    
- Use **endocytosis** to take up bone fragments
    

## ⭐ 9. **Compact Bone Microstructure**

- **Osteon (Haversian system)** = functional unit
    
- **Haversian canal** → blood vessels + nerves
    
- **Volkmann’s canals** → connect osteons
    
- **Lamellae**:
    
    - Concentric
        
    - Interstitial
        
    - Circumferential
        

## ⭐ 10. **Bone Modelling vs Remodelling**

### **Modelling**

- Shapes bone during growth
    
- Formation & resorption occur **independently**
    

### **Remodelling**

- Lifelong turnover
    
- Phases: **activation → resorption → reversal → resting**
    
- Cortical turnover: **~5%/yr**
    
- Trabecular turnover: **~15%/yr**
    
- Maintains shape, repairs microdamage, adapts to stress
    

## ⭐ 11. **Bone Pathology**

- **Osteoporosis** → excessive resorption > formation
    
- **Osteonecrosis** → bone death (e.g., medication‑related)
    
- **Edentulous** → lacking teeth
    
- **Alveolar bone loss** → tooth mobility, implant need
