# Segmentation
- **Segmentation** in embryonic development is crucial for creating ==**modular body structures**.==
- It enables the **organized formation** of features like **vertebrae** and associated muscles.
- Proper segmentation ensures ==**functional integration**== and ==**structural stability**== of the body.
	- not just for bones btw
		- vertebrae, muscles, nerves, bones

# Somitogenesis
 >The process of forming somites from ==paraxial mesoderm,== which develop into segmented body structures.
