(the numbers are stages)
## Context
- Meiosis in gonads produce haploid gametes
- Females release one egg each menstrual cycle
- Males produce 2 thousand sperm per second

# Zygote Formation
The haploid gametes meet in **fallopian tubes**
1. Which gives rise to a single zygote cell (diploid)
2. The zygote will divide into 2, then 4, then 8, then 16
	1. the division starts about 24 to 30 hours after fertilisation
	2. divides ==again== every 24 hours

>Can say either "divides" or "cleaves"
# Morulla
16 cell morulla cell with a ==lobulated surface==

> It is at this stage that the embryo moves into the ==uterine cavity== and is embedded into the ==uterine wall==

**THE MORULLA HAS NOT SEEN ANY GROWTH YET** --> Because there is no **space** for nutrients to enter

# Zona Pelucida
Surrounds the zygote
--> prevents the zygote from implanting elsewhere

e.g. if implants into fallopian tubes --> ectopic pregnancy

- the zona pelucida dissolves off after [[2. Implantation|implanting]] into uterine wall
