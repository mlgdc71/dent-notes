# Embryonic
The ==embryonic stage== occurs only in the 1st trimester and it is the stage where most **organogenesis** occurs

>organogensis is the body's initial development of various tissues, organs and limbs

- ==Face and orofacial structure formation occurs here==
	- including primary teeth buds

# Fetal
In the fetal stage that follows (2nd and 3rd trimester), only growth (**exponential growth**) occurs as all organs and tissues have formed during embryonic stage
