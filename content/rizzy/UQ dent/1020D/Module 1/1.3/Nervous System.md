- The **ectoderm** is the embryonic layer responsible for developing the **nervous system**.
- The process called **neurulation** transforms the ectoderm into the neural tube, which becomes the brain and spinal cord.
- The **mesoderm** gives rise to other structures like muscles, bones, and the circulatory system, **not** the nervous system.
- A common mistake is to confuse which germ layer leads to the nervous system.

#### How to Remember

- Think of the word **"Ecto-derm"**: **Ecto** sounds like **"exit"** — it’s the **outer** layer, where the **nervous system** originates.
- Remember **"Neurulation"** as the process that molds the ectoderm into neural structures. 😊
