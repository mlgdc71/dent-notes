# ⭐ HIGH‑YIELD NOTES — TOOTH DEVELOPMENT & ERUPTION

## ⭐ 1. **Core Stages of Tooth Development**

- **Initiation** → dental lamina forms
    
- **Bud stage** → epithelial proliferation
    
- **Cap stage** → enamel organ, dental papilla, dental follicle appear
    
- **Bell stage** → morphodifferentiation + histodifferentiation
    
- **Apposition** → dentinogenesis then amelogenesis
    
- **Calcification** → mineral deposition
    
- **Root development** → HERS forms root
    
- **Eruption** → tooth enters oral cavity
    
- **Attachment** → PDL functional
    

## ⭐ 2. **Tooth Germ Components**

- **Enamel organ** → enamel
    
- **Dental papilla** → dentin + pulp
    
- **Dental follicle/sac** → cementum, PDL, alveolar bone
    

## ⭐ 3. **Embryology**

- **Enamel** → ectoderm
    
- **Dentin, pulp, cementum, PDL** → neural crest–derived ectomesenchyme
    
- Tooth formation begins **day 37**
    
- Primary teeth start forming **week 6 in utero**
    
- Permanent teeth begin **after birth**
    

## ⭐ 4. **Enamel Essentials**

- **96% mineral** (hydroxyapatite)
    
- Formed by **ameloblasts** (from IEE)
    
- **No cells, no nerves** → no pain
    
- Enamel rods = made by **one ameloblast each**
    
- Interglobular spaces = weak points
    
- Enamel is **brittle** → relies on dentin support
    
- REE fuses with oral epithelium during eruption
    

## ⭐ 5. **Dentin Essentials**

- Formed by **odontoblasts**
    
- **Primary dentin** → initial formation
    
- **Secondary dentin** → lifelong, narrows pulp
    
- **Tertiary dentin** → injury response
    
- Dentin = **shock absorber** for enamel
    
- Tubule fluid movement → stimulates odontoblasts → pain
    

## ⭐ 6. **Pulp Essentials**

- Soft tissue with nerves + blood vessels
    
- Functions: nutrition, sensation, defense
    
- **Pulp horns** extend toward cusps → risk during cavity prep
    
- Pulp shrinks with age due to secondary dentin
    

## ⭐ 7. **Cementum & Periodontium**

- Cementum formed by **cementoblasts**
    
- Thickens at apex over time
    
- **PDL** → shock absorber, allows ~0.25 mm movement
    
- Ankylosis = tooth fused to bone (loss of PDL)
    

## ⭐ 8. **Root Development**

- Controlled by **Hertwig’s epithelial root sheath (HERS)**
    
- HERS → root shape, number, length
    
- Rests of Malassez = remnants → can form cysts
    
- Eruption occurs when **~50% of root formed**
    

## ⭐ 9. **Tooth Eruption**

### **Primary eruption**

- Begins **~6 months**
    
- Range: **6 months – 3 years**
    
- Pattern:
    
    1. Central incisors
        
    2. Lateral incisors
        
    3. Canines
        
    4. First molars
        
    5. Second molars
        

### **Permanent eruption**

- First molar at **6 years**
    
- Pattern (maxilla): **6 – 1 – 2 – 4 – 5 – 3 – 7 – 8**
    
- Pattern (mandible): **6 – 1 – 2 – 3 – 4 – 5 – 7 – 8**
    
- Third molars: **17–21 years**
    

### **Eruption forces**

- Root growth
    
- Intraosseous pressure
    
- Bone resorption
    
- Gubernacular canal guides eruption
    

## ⭐ 10. **Tooth Anatomy Essentials**

- **Anatomic crown** = enamel‑covered
    
- **Clinical crown** = visible
    
- **Anatomic root** = cementum‑covered
    
- **CEJ** = enamel–cementum junction
    
- **Cusp tip** = enamel + dentin meet
    
- **Mammelons** = incisal ridges from lobes
    

## ⭐ 11. **Developmental Processes**

- **Reciprocal induction** → dentin forms first → signals enamel formation
    
- **Morphodifferentiation** → cell shape determines crown shape
    
- **Dental lamina degeneration** → end of crown formation
    
- **Successional lamina** → permanent successors
    

## ⭐ 12. **Signaling Molecules**

- **BMPs, FGFs, Wnts, Hedgehog** pathways regulate morphogenesis
    

## ⭐ 13. **Eruption Problems & Anomalies**

- **Primary failure of eruption** → PDL absence
    
- **Ankylosis** → fusion to bone
    
- **Hypoplasia, missing teeth, supernumerary teeth**
    
- Most commonly missing:
    
    1. Third molars
        
    2. Second premolars
        
    3. Lateral incisors
        
- **Cleidocranial dysplasia** → supernumerary teeth
    

## ⭐ 14. **Wear & Adaptation**

- **Attrition** = tooth‑to‑tooth wear
    
- Cementum deposition maintains tooth length
    
- Post‑eruptive movement continues lifelong
