FOUR COMMON DISEASES

After successfully completing this course you should be able to:

1. Describe in detail the anatomy and histology of structures within and around the oral cavity, including their clinical appearance using appropriate terms.
2. Demonstrate an understanding of the basic biologic sciences relevant to dentistry, including embryology at a fundamental level as well as normal tooth development and eruption.
3. Understand the role of both microbes and saliva in the oral environment, in both health and common
dental diseases and conditions.

4. Demonstrate an understanding of the basic physical and chemical sciences relevant to dentistry, including
physical forces and acid-base and solubility equilibria.
5.Use contemporary information technologies to critically identify and evaluate scientific evidence while
contributing effectively to collaborative teamwork.
